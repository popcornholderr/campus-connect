Turn on location

Campus Connect only works with GPS on — it's how your pin shows up for classmates in real time. When the browser asks, choose Allow (or Allow while visiting the site) so this works every time you have the app open.

Your position only updates while Campus Connect is open on screen — like any site or installed app, it can't see your location in the background after you switch away. If location is blocked, open your browser's site settings for Campus Connect and set Location to Allow.
